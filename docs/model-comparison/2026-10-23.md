## Recommendation (written by hand after reading the above)

**Keep `openai/gpt-6-luna` for now.** No candidate is a clear upgrade that works within the run as it stands.

- **`openai/gpt-6-luna-pro`** read best: the most stable from one repeat to the next (field 90.6% against 83.6%), a third fewer quote misses than Luna, and the best agreement on venues. But it is twice as slow (38 s median per page against 20 s) and costs 2.8× as much per page. The run extracts pages one after another, about 120 a week counting discovery, so it would take extraction from about 40 minutes to about 75, close to `weekly-run.sh`'s 90-minute timeout. It is the first model to try again if extraction is made concurrent.
- **`anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5`** quoted best (98.6%) and was the only model right on all four hard cases every time, at Luna's speed. But it often writes a room as the venue ("Bloch Lobby", "Tivoli Cinema", "Crown Center Shops, Level 2" where the stored venue is the Nelson-Atkins or Crown Center Shops). That would split venue pages and identity matches, and it is why it matched fewer stored records (917 against 1100). Worth trying again after a rules change that names the room case, if one is wanted anyway.
- **`google/gemini-3.1-flash-lite`**: fastest (4.5 s), but the least stable fields (66%) and the worst date agreement (77%).
- **`deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash`**: cheapest, but 13 of 168 calls failed (11 timeouts), it was slowest (55 s), and it was the worst on the hard cases.
- **`qwen/qwen3.8-flash`** (dropped mid-comparison, not in the tables): 41 of 49 calls hit the run's 2-minute page timeout.
- **`mistralai/mistral-small-2603`** (dropped, not in the tables): its only provider refused 160 of 168 calls with 429s at 3 at once, and still about 1 in 3 at one at a time with four retries.

What it says about the 10-23 failures. Luna reproduced both inside three repeats of the same page: the joined Crossroads venue quote (Secret Show unverified once) and the year-round Farmers Market read as a Nov 1–22 run (once; Luna Pro once too). So #220 and #221 are rule and check problems that any model shows sometimes, not a Luna defect that a switch would fix. Luna's 31 quote misses over 168 reads come to about 10 a run from the registry pages alone.

Next, cheapest first: land #220 and #221's rule changes, then run `pnpm compare-models --models openai/gpt-6-luna,openai/gpt-6-luna-pro,anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5` on the same saved pages (they're kept until `--refetch`), which measures the rule change and the models in one pass.
