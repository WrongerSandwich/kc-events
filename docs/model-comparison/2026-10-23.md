## Recommendation (written by hand after reading the above)

**Keep `openai/gpt-6-luna` for now.** No candidate is a clear upgrade that works within the run as it stands.

- **`openai/gpt-6-luna-pro`** read best: the most stable from one repeat to the next (field 90.6% against 83.6%), a third fewer quote misses than Luna (21 against 31), and the best agreement on venues. But it is twice as slow (38 s median per page against 20 s) and costs 2.8× as much per page. The run extracts pages one after another, about 120 a week counting discovery, and the own-page reads and curation after them are serial too. Luna Pro would take extraction alone from about 40 minutes to about 75, against `weekly-run.sh`'s 90-minute timeout. It is the first model to try again if extraction is made concurrent.
- **`anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5`** quoted best (98.6%) and was the only model right on all four hard cases every time, at Luna's speed. But it titles a bill by every act on it ("Endrid, Poets Mortis, Junkyard Royalty, (the) Medicine Theory, Jimmy Peck" where the stored title is "Endrid"), most of all on recordBar. That is why it matched fewer stored records (917 against 1100): matching is by page and title. Through the run, those titles would churn identities and duplicate events. It also often writes a room as the venue ("Bloch Lobby", "Tivoli Cinema" for the Nelson-Atkins), which would split venue pages. Both are rules questions (the title rules and a room rule) to settle before trying it again.
- **`google/gemini-3.1-flash-lite`**: much the fastest (4.5 s median), with stability level with Luna (field 83.7%) and dates that agree with the stored records 95.8% of the time. But it quotes worse (96.3%, 53 misses), costs 2.4× Luna, and writes rooms and addresses as venues ("Big Theatre" and "Main Theater" for Liberty Hall, "1801 East 18th Street" for Zhou B), the worst venue agreement of the five (89.9%).
- **`deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash`**: cheapest, but 13 of 168 calls failed (11 timeouts), it was slowest (55 s), and it was the worst on the hard cases.
- **`qwen/qwen3.8-flash`** (dropped mid-comparison, not in the tables): 41 of 49 calls hit the run's 2-minute page timeout.
- **`mistralai/mistral-small-2603`** (dropped, not in the tables): its only provider refused 160 of 168 calls with 429s at 3 at once, and about 1 in 3 even at one at a time with four retries.

What it says about the 10-23 failures. Luna reproduced both inside three repeats of the same page: the joined Crossroads venue quote (Secret Show unverified once) and the year-round Farmers Market read as a Nov 1–22 run (once; Luna Pro once too). So #220 and #221 are rule and check problems that any model shows sometimes, not a Luna defect that a switch would fix. Luna's 31 quote misses over 168 reads come to about 10 a run from the registry pages alone.

Next, cheapest first: land #220 and #221's rule changes, then run `pnpm compare-models --models openai/gpt-6-luna,openai/gpt-6-luna-pro,anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5` on the same saved pages (they're kept until `--refetch`). That measures the rule change and the models in one pass.
