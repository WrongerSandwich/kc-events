## Synthesis

**Verdict: launchable, with a short list first.** No lane found a P0. The static surfaces are sound at the level that matters to a first wave: hash-only CSP and the security headers, no injection sink for dataset text, unique canonicals and valid structured data on all 559 pages, 439 calendar files that parse clean, first-party requests only, and, most of all, every sampled date, time and venue on the 14 current picks matches its source page. What the lanes found is a layer above that: a function that trusts its form, a build cadence that leaves most of the week's pages stale, trust copy that over-claims, and a handful of editorial lines on the front page that the source pages do not support.

## Lane-by-lane headlines

- The crawl, share and calendar surfaces are sound: unique canonicals on all 559 pages, all 975 JSON-LD blocks parse, all 558 sitemap URLs answer 200, every link-preview crawler gets static tags and a 1200x630 card, and all 439 calendar files parse clean in two parsers with stable UIDs across builds.
- The P1s found so far are data, not markup: the catch-all region labels venues outside the city as "Kansas City" in JSON-LD (Powell Gardens is in Kingsville), and three of the fourteen current picks are at venues with no `data/venues.yaml` row, so the flagship don't-miss feed carries no mappable address for them.
- The one security P1: `/api/report` files whatever title and links a client sends in three hidden fields, for an event id it never checks against the dataset, so a script can create public issues on the repository with any title and any link at the firewall's per-IP rate (F1). The static site itself is clean: hash-only CSP on every page, http(s)-only links enforced twice, no HTML sink takes dataset text, first-party requests only, no cookies.
- The data holds where it matters most: every sampled date, time and venue on all 14 current picks matches its source page, no sampled event is cancelled or postponed, and 65 of 66 evidence quotes are still on their pages. What is wrong is editorial: four of the 14 why-lines claim things the page does not say (Dark Forest "three nights only" is seven nights; Graveyard 5K's "open once a year"; the Invincible Czars as "a Kansas City band"; Hedwig's "likely" in person), and the City Market is listed as a one-off through a Monday it does not run.
- Two structural P1s from the code lane: the site is built once a week, so from Tuesday on the server HTML is Monday's and hydration swaps whole sections in place (CLS measured at 0.17 to 0.49 on `/` and `/explore` later in the week; the script-free kind, region and venue pages list past events until the next build), fixed by a daily deploy hook; and a `data/venues.yaml` row whose venue's last record is archived makes the build throw, so a future weekly data commit would be refused and the site would quietly stop refreshing (earliest around January 2027). Gates are green: typecheck clean, 415 site tests pass, coverage 93.6% lines, all six size budgets pass (two with under 100 bytes to spare).
- The UX critique scores the site 28/40 (good, low end): the front page is now a good instrument (started picks fold, the week strip tells the truth about runs, trust is visible), and the rough edges are in the surfaces added since 10-10. Its P1s: the Saved view is worded as a failed filter ("No events match saved only") and hides a running save inside a closed "On now" fold; the pages frozen at the build day (the same finding as E1, with screenshots: on a Saturday `/v/liberty-hall` opens with Wednesday's show under a red "Don't miss"); and "find something for Saturday night" has no direct path (the week strip's Fri and Sat both jump to Dark Forest's Thursday card; "This weekend" opens with 44 "On now" rows; no day preset).
- The copy follows the house style everywhere (no "we", no exclamation marks, no slogans, sentence case, en dashes), but two trust claims fail where readers land: "Every date is read from the event's own page" is false (dates are read from the venue's or organizer's list page, as the event page itself says: "verified Oct 19 from the list at ..."), and nothing on the front page or an event page says a model writes the picks, reasons and descriptions, which PRODUCT.md promises the site says. Also P1: 59 explorer rows say "All day" for events whose time was simply not listed.
- Operations are in order: apex and `.html` 308 to the canonical, true 404s, no runtime errors in seven days, Node 24 pinned everywhere, the report function's rate limit is live (a second request from one address inside a few minutes got 429).

## Lane H: operations and launch (orchestrating session)

Checked by hand with curl, dig, the Vercel API (read-only) and the GitHub CLI.

### Confirmed in order

- DNS: `kcthisweek.com` A records on Vercel, CAA for letsencrypt.org, sectigo.com and pki.goog, a Google site-verification TXT.
- Vercel project `kc-events`: framework astro, `nodeVersion` 24.x (matches `.nvmrc` and `engines`), no password, SSO or trusted-IP protection, latest production deployment READY at `cb366f2`, no runtime errors in the last 7 days, apex domain redirects to www with 308. Two things this session could not read: the environment-variable list (the API token is not allowed to) and the firewall configuration (blocked by the permission classifier), so the `/api/report` rate-limit rule is confirmed only indirectly: a HEAD request from the audit's address right after a GET got 429.
- Web Analytics on the Hobby plan includes 50,000 events a month; past that, collection pauses after a three-day grace period, with no charge. A launch week on /r/kansascity should sit well inside that.
- GitHub: the repository has a description, a homepage, the wrong-listing issue form, the triage labels, and Dependabot weekly with a seven-day cooldown for npm and Actions.

### Findings

- [P3] [confirmed] HSTS has no `includeSubDomains` or `preload`, and the domain is not on the preload list (`hstspreload.org` status unknown). Location: Vercel's default header. Impact: a first visit typed as `kcthisweek.com` goes over http once. Fix: only if wanted; `includeSubDomains; preload` on both hosts in `vercel.json`, then submit at hstspreload.org. Low value for a site with no subdomains.
- [P3] [confirmed] An unknown calendar path (`/calendar/nope.ics`) returns the HTML 404 page with `Content-Type: text/calendar` (the `vercel.json` header rule matches any `.ics` path). Impact: none for a reader; a calendar app sees a 404 either way. Fix: leave it, or scope the header rule to `/calendar/(.*)\.ics` and `/e/(.*)\.ics`.
- [P3] [confirmed] `kc-events-lime.vercel.app` and the `*-wrongersandwichs-projects.vercel.app` hosts serve the site under their own names. The canonical tag points to www on every page, so search is unaffected. Fix: none needed; a redirect from the vercel.app domain is a one-line project setting if the duplicate ever shows up in Search Console.
- [P3] [confirmed] The repository's homepage field is the apex `https://kcthisweek.com` (one redirect from GitHub's link), and it has no topics. Fix: set the homepage to `https://www.kcthisweek.com` and add topics such as `kansas-city`, `events`, `astro`, `svelte`.

### Looked at, no finding

The front page, the explorer and an event page at 390 and 1280 px in both schemes read as the product brief asks: dense, legible, colour only where it means something, no AI-generated tells. Small things handed to lanes A and B: an explorer row on a phone truncates its venue and region line mid-word ("Kauffman Center for… · Cro"); the empty `Saved` view says "No events match saved only" rather than teaching the feature; the front page's "Browse all 437 events" links to `/explore?recurring=1` while `/explore` opened directly says "426 events" (the recurring rows are off by default), which is correct but is the first number a reader compares.

## Lane D: SEO, sharing, structured data, calendar feeds

Audited against `site/dist` (559 pages, 439 `.ics` files, built from the 2026-10-19 run) and the live site. Scripts: `extract.mjs`, `validate-ics.mjs`, `links.mjs`, `live.mjs`, `explore.mjs` (scratch, not kept).

### Summary

1. The crawl surface is sound: every one of the 559 pages has a unique, extensionless `https://www.` canonical with `og:url` equal to it; all 975 JSON-LD blocks parse; all 558 sitemap URLs answer 200 on live; `.html`, trailing-slash, `/index`, apex and `http` all 308 to the canonical form; an unknown event is a real 404 with `noindex`; every link-preview crawler tested gets the tags from static HTML and a 30 KB 1200x630 card.
2. All 439 calendar files (426 per-event, 13 feeds) and the explorer export parse clean in ical.js and node-ical: CRLF, 75-octet folding (multibyte safe), UTC instants so no VTIMEZONE is needed, all-day runs with exclusive ends, the one weekday RRULE expanding to exactly the nine dates it should, UIDs identical between Vercel's build and the local one.
3. What falls short is place and description data rather than markup: 73 of the 421 Event objects (and 3 of the 14 picks in the flagship don't-miss feed) are at venues with no `data/venues.yaml` row, so they carry a city-only address, four of them a wrong one ("Kansas City" for Powell Gardens in Kingsville); Event `url` points off-site on all 421; 250 Events and most meta descriptions have no description beyond a date line; `llms.txt` and the internal link graph leave the landing pages, venue pages and feeds hard to find.

### Findings

#### D1. [P1 fix before launch] [confirmed] The catch-all region puts venues outside Kansas City in "Kansas City"

- Location: `site/src/lib/event-meta.ts:20` (`postalAddress`: `if (region === undefined) return { addressLocality: "Kansas City" }`), `site/src/site.config.ts` `regionAddress` (no entry for "Elsewhere in the metro"), test "states no state for the catch-all, which spans both" in `site/test/event-meta.test.ts`.
- Impact: a crawler reads a false postal locality for an event, including the pick Dark Forest at Powell Gardens, which is in Kingsville, MO, 30 miles east of the city. Google's Event guidance asks for the detailed address of where the event is held; a wrong city is worse than none.
- Evidence: 4 Events carry `{"addressLocality":"Kansas City","addressCountry":"US"}` with no state: `/e/evt_41a40a81ea8a` (Powell Gardens), `/e/evt_526f54f1d2e7` (Elmwood Cemetery), `/e/evt_5edb3a05d203` (Tony Aguirre Community Center), `/e/evt_77d82b07e57d` ("Offsite Location"). The ICS for the same events reads `LOCATION:Powell Gardens\, Elsewhere in the metro`.
- Fix: for the catch-all region return no locality at all (`addressCountry` only, or omit `address` and keep `location.name`), and add `venues.yaml` rows for its real venues (Powell Gardens: 1609 NW US Hwy 50, Kingsville, MO 64061), which `postalAddressLd` then uses in full. The ICS path should likewise drop the neighborhood when it equals the catch-all region name.

#### D2. [P1 fix before launch] [confirmed] Three of the fourteen picks have no mappable address in JSON-LD or the don't-miss feed

- Location: `data/venues.yaml` (40 rows); `site/src/lib/ics.ts:88` (`LOCATION` falls back to `venue, neighborhood`); `site/src/lib/event-meta.ts:72`.
- Impact: the feed the front page asks readers to subscribe to carries `LOCATION:Union Cemetery\, Midtown`, `LOCATION:Elmwood Cemetery\, East Bottoms` and `LOCATION:Powell Gardens\, Elsewhere in the metro`, so a phone cannot map them, and no `GEO`. The same three Event objects have no `streetAddress` or `geo`.
- Evidence: 57 venues with published events have no row; 77 of 437 events (17.6%) are at them; 73 of 421 Event objects lack `streetAddress`; 228 of the 1,292 VEVENTs across all files lack a street address. Picks at row-less venues: Dark Forest (Powell Gardens), Electric Poe (Union Cemetery), Graveyard 5K (Elmwood Cemetery). Largest row-less venues by event count: Cable Dahmer Arena 6, Spencer Museum of Art 3, Unicorn Theatre 3.
- Fix: add rows for the three picks' venues now; then a build-time warning (or a line in the run report) naming every pick whose venue has no row, so the weekly cron surfaces the next one.

#### D3. [P2 next pass] [confirmed] Event JSON-LD `url` is the venue's page on all 421 events, and there is no `@id`

- Location: `site/src/lib/event-meta.ts:78` (`url: detailsUrl(e)`), `site/src/lib/events.ts` `detailsUrl`.
- Impact: schema.org `url` is "URL of the item", and consumers (Google, and the ItemList on `/` and `/k/*`, `/r/*`, which list `/e/<id>` URLs) take it as the entity's canonical page. Every Event here says its page is `lied.ku.edu/...`, `thecomedyclubkc.com/...` (20 distinct hosts), while the BreadcrumbList on the same page ends at `/e/<id>`. The site's own page is the one carrying the markup; pointing `url` elsewhere hands the entity to the venue's domain and makes the two lists on the front and landing pages inconsistent with the Events they summarise.
- Evidence: `Event url == page url: 0` of 421; `@id: 0`. Sample: `/e/evt_87ced2675ef5` has `"url":"https://lied.ku.edu/event/david-sedaris/"` and `offers.url` the same.
- Fix: `url` and `@id` = `${origin}/e/${id}`; keep the venue's page in `offers.url` (already) and add it as `sameAs` so the link-out is not lost.

#### D4. [P2 next pass] [confirmed] Most Event objects lack the recommended `description`, `endDate`, `offers`, `organizer`, `performer`

- Location: `site/src/lib/event-meta.ts:60-82`.
- Impact: Google treats these as recommended for Event rich results; thin events rank below fuller ones and show less in the result. Not a validity problem.
- Evidence (of 421): `description` 171 (250 missing), `endDate` 95 (267 timed starts with no end, 72 date-only ends, 23 timed ends), `offers` 6 (none with `availability` or `validFrom`), `organizer` 0, `performer` 0, `image` 421 but always the site's generic `og.png`. The 6 offers are correct (`Offer` with `price` 11, `AggregateOffer` 35 to 50 from "$35–$50 Adult"; "Pay what you can" does not occur in the current data).
- Fix: `description` should fall back to the meta description's date-and-place sentence when the page has no description text, so none is empty; `organizer` as the venue (`Organization` with `name` and `url` from the venue row) where the venue is the host, which it is for nearly every source in the registry; do not invent `endDate` (the ICS two-hour placeholder is right for a calendar, wrong for structured data). `offers` will grow only as the own-page read covers more events.

#### D5. [P2 next pass] [confirmed] Event meta descriptions never name the event and for 250 pages are a date line alone

- Location: `site/src/lib/event-meta.ts:7-11` (`eventDescription`: date line, venue, neighborhood, then the why-line if any).
- Impact: a search snippet or a Slack/iMessage preview under a title such as "Floating Pumpkin Patch" reads "Sunday, October 25 at Screenland Armour, North Kansas City." for every Screenland film that night; 36 descriptions are under 50 characters and 14 groups of pages share one description word for word. The page's own description text (180 events have one) is never used here, only in JSON-LD.
- Evidence: duplicates include "Sunday, November 1 at Screenland Armour, North Kansas City." (3 pages), "Friday, October 23, 8:00 pm at Knuckleheads, East Bottoms." (3), "Through Saturday, October 24 at Lawrence Arts Center, Lawrence." (3). 13 descriptions exceed 160 characters (max 247), all pick pages where the why-line runs long.
- Fix: `${kind or title}: ${date line} at ${venue}. ${description text, trimmed to about 110 chars, else why-line}`. Titles are already distinct except four same-named pairs (see D9).

#### D6. [P2 next pass] [confirmed] `llms.txt` leaves out the feeds, the landing and venue pages, the query grammar, the dataset fields and the picks

- Location: `site/src/pages/llms.txt.ts`.
- Impact: a chatbot reading it learns of `/`, `/explore`, `/e/<id>`, `/about`, `/events.json`, the sitemap and the repo, and nothing else. It cannot build a filtered explorer link (keys `ids, when, kind, region, dontmiss, recurring, saved, q, sort` live only in `src/lib/query.ts`), does not know `/calendar.ics`, `/calendar/dont-miss.ics` or `/calendar/<kind>.ics` exist, nor `/changes`, `/venues`, `/v/<slug>`, `/k/<kind>`, `/r/<region>`, and gets no event at all (not the week's picks, not even their titles). The 2026-10-10 audit's idea 47 (document the query grammar) is still open.
- Evidence: live `/llms.txt` is 19 lines: H1, blockquote, one paragraph, "## Pages" with 4 links, "## Data" with 3 links. Spec-wise the shape (H1, blockquote, H2 sections of links) is right.
- Fix: add sections for Calendar feeds (three URL patterns, webcal note), Lists (`/changes`, `/venues`, the 11 kind and 8 region pages, with their current counts), the explorer's query grammar (one line per key with its values and an example), the `events.json` fields (the `PublishedEvent` keys and `schemaVersion`), and the current picks as a short list with their `/e/` links so the file is useful on its own.

#### D7. [P2 next pass] [confirmed] The kind, region and venue index pages are not linked from the front page or the explorer

- Location: `site/src/pages/index.astro`, `site/src/components/Footer.astro` (links: `/about`, `/changes`, `/events.json`, GitHub), `site/src/pages/v/[slug].astro:34` (the comment that a footer link to `/venues` was left out for the page budgets).
- Impact: `/k/*` is reached only through an event page's kind chip (depth 2 for 7 kinds, 3 for 4), `/r/*` only through the landing pages' own "By region" line (depth 3 for all 8), `/venues` only through a venue page's "All venues" (depth 3). The sitemap lists them, so they are crawled, but with almost no internal link equity and no anchor text on the page a reader lands on; the /r/kansascity visitor who wants "comedy" or "Johnson County" has no visible way there from `/`.
- Evidence: `links.mjs`: from `/`, 0 links to `/k/`, 0 to `/r/`, 0 to `/v/`; `/venues` has 97 inbound links, all from `/v/*`; 31 distinct `/explore?...` variants are linked site-wide, each canonicalising to `/explore` (good, no crawl-budget issue).
- Fix: one "By kind" row of the 11 kind links under the picks on `/` (or in the footer, text only, well under the 200 B budget steps the project has accepted before) and "By region" beside it; `/venues` in the footer. The explorer's kind chips could also be links to `/k/<kind>` for the no-JS case.

#### D8. [P2 next pass] [plausible] The subscribe link is `webcal:` only; on Android and Windows that scheme has no handler

- Location: `site/src/components/Subscribe.astro`, `site/src/pages/about.astro:36` (the "everything listed" and per-kind links, also `webcal:`).
- Impact: iOS and macOS open the Calendar subscribe dialog from `webcal://www.kcthisweek.com/calendar/dont-miss.ics`. Chrome on Android and most Windows browsers show an "unable to open" message or nothing. Those readers have to select and copy the `https` address from a `<code>` span and paste it into Google Calendar's "From URL". The copy does say "If it asks for an address, use ...", which is honest, but the primary control fails silently for roughly half of phones.
- Evidence: rendered About copy: "Subscribe to the picks in your calendar app. It updates weekly; remove the calendar to stop. If it asks for an address, use https://www.kcthisweek.com/calendar/dont-miss.ics." and "Or subscribe to everything listed, or to one kind: music, theater and dance, ..." with every `href` `webcal://`. Not tested on a device.
- Fix: offer both: "Subscribe (Apple)" as `webcal:` and "Copy address for Google or Outlook" as a one-tap copy of the `https` URL (the ShareButton's copy path already exists), plus a short line "Google Calendar: Other calendars, From URL".

## Lane C: security and the trust boundary

Audited at `cb366f2` (the live site's `last-modified` matches that commit's deploy), read-only. Harnesses: `report-harness.ts` and `changes-check.ts` (scratch, not kept). Numbered F1 to F10 as the lane's harness output names them.

### Summary

1. The static site is clean: a hash-only meta CSP in `<head>` on every page, http(s)-only links enforced at the dataset and again at projection, no HTML sink takes dataset text, ICS, JSON-LD and sitemap escaping hold under hostile input, and the live site makes only first-party requests with no cookies and no storage until a reader saves something.
2. The one function, `/api/report`, handles the reader's text, the token, errors, cross-site posts and the back link correctly, but it trusts three hidden fields (title, primary page, event page) and never checks the event id against the dataset: a scripted client can file public issues on the owner's repository with any title and any link, at the firewall's per-IP rate.
3. Before launch: derive the event's context server-side from its id (F1, small change). Next pass: a global cap or dedupe inside the function so flood protection does not rest on the firewall alone (F2), the vite 8.3.3 bump (F3), and the one changes-page href that bypasses the projection's scheme guard (F4).

### Findings

#### F1. [P1 fix before launch] [confirmed] The report function files whatever title and links a client sends, for an event id it never checks

- Location: `site/src/server/report.ts`, `parseReport` (the event-context branch) and `issueFor`; `site/src/components/ReportForm.astro`, the hidden inputs `title`, `event`, `primary-page`, `event-page`.
- Impact: anyone with curl (no `Origin` header, which the function accepts by design so that older browsers and the no-JavaScript path work) can create issues on `github.com/WrongerSandwich/kc-events` titled `Wrong listing: <any text up to 500 characters>` whose body carries an arbitrary http(s) URL under "Primary page" and "Event page", autolinked by GitHub, labelled `bug` and `needs-triage`. The repository is public and indexed, so its issue list becomes a link-spam and phishing surface under the owner's name, and spam filed through the owner's token is spam from the owner's account in GitHub's eyes. The firewall rule bounds this per IP only (the wizard specifies 5 per 10 minutes), so one address gets up to 720 issues a day and more addresses scale linearly.
- Evidence (local harness against the handler with GitHub faked):

  ```
  POST what=Totally legit report
       &title=Free crypto giveaway, click the primary page
       &event=https://www.kcthisweek.com/e/evt_doesnotexist
       &primary-page=https://evil.example/phish?utm=kc
       &event-page=https://evil.example/@WrongerSandwich/[x](y)
  -> 200, filed 1
     TITLE: "Wrong listing: Free crypto giveaway, click the primary page"
     BODY:  ### Event on kcthisweek.com
            https://www.kcthisweek.com/e/evt_doesnotexist
            ### Primary page
            https://evil.example/phish?utm=kc
            ### Event page
            https://evil.example/@WrongerSandwich/[x](y)
  ```

  A 300-character title containing `@WrongerSandwich **bold** [x](https://evil.example) #1` also filed (378-character issue title). The `what` text itself is correctly fenced and cannot inject.
- Fix: take only the event id from the client and look the rest up in the build's own data. `src/server/report.ts` already imports with `.js` extensions that Vercel resolves to `.ts`, so the generated events module works the same way:

  ```ts
  import { events } from "../generated/events.js";
  // in parseReport, after the site-origin and /e/<id> path checks on `event`:
  const published = events.find((e) => e.id === site.pathname.slice("/e/".length));
  if (published === undefined) return { status: 400 };
  return {
    what,
    context: { title: published.title, event: eventUrl, primaryUrl: published.primaryUrl, ...(published.eventUrl && { eventUrl: published.eventUrl }) },
  };
  ```

  Then drop `title`, `primary-page` and `event-page` from `FIELDS` and from the form's hidden inputs (the GitHub fallback link in `lib/report.ts` is built at render time and is unaffected). If the generated module's ~200 KB inside the function is unwelcome, fetch `/events.json` from the canonical origin at request time instead; the import is simpler and has no runtime dependency. Either way an unknown id is refused, and the only reader-controlled text in the issue stays inside the fence.

#### F2. [P2 next pass] [confirmed] Flood protection rests entirely on a firewall rule outside the repository

- Location: `site/src/server/report.ts`, `handleReport`; `scripts/report-form-setup.sh` stage 3. `vercel.json` has no rate limit and the function keeps no state.
- Impact: what the repository alone guarantees is a field whitelist, refusal of repeated fields, a 16 KB body cap, a 2,000-character `what`, a honeypot (meaningless to a script, which leaves it empty), and refusal of browser posts from other origins. It guarantees nothing about volume: without the firewall rule a script could file at GitHub's content-creation limit (hundreds per hour) into `needs-triage`, and there is no dedupe, so one event can be reported a thousand times. The rule is live and applies per IP only; its numbers live in the dashboard, not in the repo.
- Evidence: harness `cross-site: no Origin, no Sec-Fetch-Site (curl) -> 200, filed 1`; live `OPTIONS /api/report -> HTTP/2 429` with `x-vercel-mitigated: deny` on this host's third request to the path.
- Fix (cheap, stateless, one extra GitHub call per report; Search needs only the Issues read the token has): before filing, ask GitHub how many issues the token's user opened in the last hour and refuse past a ceiling, and reuse an open issue for the same event:

  ```
  GET /search/issues?q=repo:WrongerSandwich/kc-events+is:issue+author:@me+created:>=<ISO one hour ago>
      -> total_count >= 20 ? answer 429, file nothing
  GET /search/issues?q=repo:WrongerSandwich/kc-events+is:issue+is:open+label:needs-triage+"<event URL>"+in:body
      -> found ? POST a comment on it instead of a new issue
  ```

  The ceiling turns a flood into at most 20 issues an hour across all IPs; the dedupe keeps repeated reports as one thread. Also record the firewall rule's numbers in AGENTS.md so a reader knows what the edge guarantees.

#### F4. [P3 next pass] [confirmed] The changes page links gone events' pages without the projection's scheme guard

#### F5. [P3 polish] [confirmed] A long report in a three-byte script is refused with no reason

#### F8. [P3 polish] [confirmed] Every static response carries `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`, not only `/events.json`; no COOP or CORP

### Sound, keep as is

CSP and headers (live, every page type fetched: `/`, `/explore`, `/about`, `/404`, `/e/<id>`, `/venues`, `/changes`):

- The meta CSP is in `<head>` on every page: `default-src 'self'; img-src 'self' data:; object-src 'none'; base-uri 'self'; form-action 'self'; script-src 'self' 'sha256-...'...; style-src 'self' 'sha256-...'...; style-src-attr 'unsafe-inline'`. `script-src` is hash-only with no `'unsafe-inline'`, and every inline script's SHA-256 on every page matched a listed hash (the explore slice script, the analytics snippet, each island bootstrap). JSON-LD blocks are unhashed, which is right: `type="application/ld+json"` is data, not script. The preview toolbar branch (`vercel.live`, pusher, `'unsafe-inline'` styles) is gated on `VERCEL_ENV === "preview"` and none of it appears in production.
- `style-src-attr 'unsafe-inline'` covers the 1 to 20 `style=` attributes per page (view-transition names, CSS variables). An attacker needs HTML injection first, which the templating denies; the right trade.
- The missing `connect-src` is fine: it falls back to `default-src 'self'`, and the analytics beacon is same-origin (observed with an empty console: no CSP report). `frame-src`, `worker-src` and `manifest-src` fall back to `'self'`; nothing uses them.
- `vercel.json`'s `/(.*)` block lands on every page and on the function: `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, `Permissions-Policy: camera=(), geolocation=(), microphone=()`, `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, and the header CSP `frame-ancestors 'none'` (correctly a header, since a meta policy ignores that directive). `.ics` is `text/calendar; charset=utf-8`; `/events.json` is `public, max-age=3600` with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` as intended; HTML is `public, max-age=0, must-revalidate`.

The function, `/api/report`:

- Only `POST` is exported; Vercel answers 405 to GET and HEAD before the handler runs. `/api/Report` is 404, `/api/report/` and `/api/report.ts` 308 to `/api/report`, `/api/report.js` is 404. The firewall rule is live on the path.
- The reader's text is wrapped in a backtick fence one longer than any run of backticks in it: `@WrongerSandwich`, `#129`, `### What is wrong`, `<img onerror>`, `![img](...)`, `<!-- -->`, RTL and zero-width characters, and a line of four backticks all stayed literal. The issue goes to GitHub as JSON, so CRLF in the text cannot touch any header.
- URLs are parsed with `new URL`, limited to http(s), refused with credentials, and re-serialized (`"`, `<`, `>`, a newline and U+2028 come out percent-encoded or dropped). The site-page URL must be on a known origin with path `/e/[\w-]+` and no query or fragment: `?x=1`, `#y`, `/e/../about`, `/E/`, the apex, a trailing dot and a trailing slash were all refused; `:443` normalizes.
- Cross-site refusal: `Sec-Fetch-Site: cross-site`, or an `Origin` outside the deployment's own and canonical origins, is 403. A third-party page's top-level form POST carries both `Origin: https://evil.example` and `Sec-Fetch-Site: cross-site` in every current browser, so it is refused; `Origin: null` (sandboxed frame, `data:` page), the apex, a sibling subdomain and a stray `*.vercel.app` origin are refused too. A missing Origin (curl, a typed URL) is accepted, which is the design and why F1 and F2 matter. The content type is not checked and need not be: the body is read as form encoding whatever the header says, and the Origin check is what stops cross-site posts.
- No open redirect: the plain page's back link is a validated `/e/<id>` path from the context or from a same-origin Referer, else `/about`; there is no `Location` header anywhere. A `javascript:` or foreign Referer falls to `/about`.
- Errors: GitHub 401, 422 and 500 and a network failure all become `502 {"ok":false}` (or the plain page) with no upstream text; a missing token is 503; the token never appears in a response (checked) and is never logged (logs carry GitHub's status and 300 characters of its body, or a fetch error message). A malformed body (JSON, binary, bad percent-escapes, empty, unknown or repeated keys, `__proto__`, a case variant of a field name) is 400 and files nothing.
- The handler's own headers: `Cache-Control: no-store`, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`, `Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'none'; form-action 'self'; frame-ancestors 'none'; base-uri 'none'`. The plain page escapes `& < > " '` in the GitHub link and the back link.
- Body cap 16 KB, `what` 2,000 characters, title 500, a field whitelist, a honeypot, and the token read from `process.env` at request time only. The e2e build sets a sentinel token and asserts no built file contains it; a grep of `dist/` found no token names and no source maps.

### Anti-patterns verdict

It mostly passes; a Linear or Fantastical user would sit down with it. There are no gradient text, glassmorphism, hero metrics, side-stripe borders, decorative illustrations or modals. The only display face (Josefin Sans) is confined to the wordmark. The logo's fountain animation is a small hover moment that honours reduced motion; acceptable.

What does read as generated or off:
- **Ten kind hues at the same lightness and chroma, spaced evenly round the wheel.** It is mechanical rather than designed. "Don't miss" red (hue 27) sits 7 degrees from talks/readings (hue 20), so the one state colour shares a family with a kind (`site/src/styles/tokens.css`).
- **Eight identical cards in a row on the front page.** Each has a tile, a three-icon fact line, a muted why line, a chip, a host link and two 44 px outlined buttons. On a phone the page is 5,015 px (it was 4,911 on 10-10).
- **Save is not one component.** Four looks and one absence: card (44 px outlined box, ink label, pressed is solid black with "✓ Saved"); explorer desktop row (a 20 px slim box); event page (same box as the card, but with muted grey text beside full-ink "Share" and "Add to calendar", so it reads as the disabled one); phone explorer row (a 44 px box on every one of 426 rows, so the right edge becomes a column of "Save"); kind, region, venue and changes rows (no Save at all, and on desktop an empty column where it would be).
- **Calendar has three renderings:** an icon-only square on desktop cards, "Calendar" on phone cards, "Add to calendar" on the event page.
- **The kind chip has three behaviours with one look:** a filter toggle above the front page's sections; an inert span on cards and rows; an underlined link inside the pill on the event page, the only underlined chip anywhere.

## Lane A: technical frontend quality

All measurements were run by scripts against the local preview (`site/dist`, 559 pages) and, for performance, against production. Numbered A1 to A20 here; the lane's own harness names them F1 to F20.

### Summary

- **Scores:** Accessibility 3, Performance 3, Theming 4, Responsive 3, Anti-patterns 4. **Total 17/20.**
- **Strong:** the token system holds up under measurement. Every text pair is at least 5.03:1 in light and 5.99:1 in dark, the focus ring is 4.23:1 / 8.78:1, and reduced motion is fully honoured. There are no hard-coded colours, no anti-pattern tells, and the home page weighs 96 KB with LCP at 288 ms.
- **Must fix before launch (P1):** three confirmed defects: A1, the explorer's sticky bar fully hides the focused row when tabbing backwards (WCAG 2.4.11, AA); A2, two event pages scroll sideways at 320 px (WCAG 1.4.10); A3, the week-strip icons spill into the neighbouring day's tile at iPhone widths.
- **Biggest P2s:** nothing for `forced-colors`, 326 crowded sub-24 px touch targets in the explorer at 390 px, a heavy `/explore` (8,163 DOM nodes), and truncated titles on desktop.

### P1 (fix before launch)

#### A1. [P1] [confirmed] Focus is entirely hidden under the explorer's sticky bar when tabbing backwards. WCAG 2.4.11 (AA, new in 2.2)

- Location: `site/src/components/Explorer.svelte:220` (`.bar`, sticky, `top: 0`); `site/src/styles/base.css:89-92` (`.section-heading.group`, sticky under the bar); `Explorer.svelte:273` (`.collapsed[open] summary`, also sticky). Only `.results` has a `scroll-margin-top`; there is no `scroll-padding-top` anywhere.
- Evidence: tabbed down 70 stops on `/explore` at 1280x900, then Shift+Tab through 25 stops. 6 were entirely obscured (all 9 sample points covered), 3 more partly. Examples: the "SUNN O)))" link at top=43 px and a "Save" at top=20 px, with the bar's bottom at 47 px and the sticky day heading below it. #### A2. [P1] [confirmed] Event pages overflow horizontally at 320 px when "Don't miss" sits beside a long kind label. WCAG 1.4.10 (AA)

#### A3. [P1] [confirmed] Week strip: a day with 4 or more picks spills its icons out of the tile at phone widths from 385 px up

### Positive findings

- Token system: `tokens.css` is a single source for every colour in both schemes; contrast is comfortable everywhere, and dark mode has real parity rather than inversion.
- Focus: a global 2 px `:focus-visible` ring with 4.23:1 / 8.78:1 contrast, padded inside overflow-hidden rows so it is not clipped.
- Chips and live regions: chips are real `<button aria-pressed>`; live regions are hidden `role=status` paragraphs seeded on mount so they do not announce on load, with a 500 ms debounce on the count. Removing a filter pill hands focus to the neighbour.
- Reduced motion: thorough, including view transitions and smooth scroll.
- Layout robustness: no horizontal overflow at 390 px or wider on any tested page, and none at 320 px except A2. Zoom to 200% and 400% reflows cleanly. Long titles and venues wrap.
- Weight: pages are 83 to 96 KB, with no third-party requests and a first-party analytics script. Geist is preloaded with `crossorigin`; latin subsets only.
- Semantics: one h1 per page, correct landmark names, kind never by colour alone, no images without alt. Anti-patterns: none; the detector found 0 issues in `src`. Pinch zoom is allowed; Save, Share and Add to calendar are 44 px.

Real devices: iOS Dynamic Type, real touch accuracy and mobile Safari's sticky behaviour.
