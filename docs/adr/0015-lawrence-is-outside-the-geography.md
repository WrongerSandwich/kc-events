# Lawrence is outside the geography

From the first proposal the geography was "the KC metro on both sides of the state line, plus Lawrence for concerts", a default Evan could veto at build time. It grew past concerts: by 2026-11-20 the registry had seven Lawrence sources (The Bottleneck, The Granada, Replay Lounge, the Lied Center of Kansas, the Lawrence Arts Center, the Spencer Museum of Art and Liberty Hall), Lawrence was a region of its own with a page, and 54 of the site's 423 published events were there, a film series and a museum's gallery talks among them, one of them a pick.

Evan vetoed it on 2026-11-20 (#285): a site named for Kansas City that lists a town forty miles west misleads the reader who came for Kansas City. Lawrence is now outside the geography, as Topeka and St. Joseph always were. The geography line reads "Kansas City metro on both sides of the state line"; the sources, the region, its venue rows and its entry in the site's address table are gone; the extraction rules tell the model a Lawrence venue or address, the University of Kansas's Lawrence campus included, is `outsideGeography`.

Taking a region off the list is not enough on its own, because of two things the job does for good reasons. A stored neighborhood the list no longer holds is read as a rename and placed again, in the catch-all when nothing better is known, so every stored Lawrence event would have stayed published under "Elsewhere in the metro". And the model's flag is one model's word: a Kansas City venue's page that lists its promoter's Lawrence show can come back with a neighborhood of "Lawrence" and the flag false, which would land in the catch-all as an unmappable neighborhood. So the config says it outright, in a key of its own, `outsidePlaces`, and the run holds to it in three places:

- **Extraction.** A reading whose neighborhood is an outside place is dropped and counted with the readings dropped as outside the geography, whatever its flag said.
- **Stored events.** Before this run's sightings merge, every stored event not yet expired whose neighborhood is an outside place is expired under a new reason, `outside-geography`. The midweek re-verification does the same. It is never moved to the catch-all, and an event held for promotion loses its hold with its status, as any expired event does.
- **Sightings.** A sighting that matches an event expired as `outside-geography` is passed over and counted as outside, so a page that keeps listing the event cannot bring it back under another neighborhood, the one case the first rule misses. The expired record is what remembers the call, until it is archived eight weeks after its date.

A place is matched whole, in any case and punctuation, so the config lists each form a page may write ("Lawrence", "Lawrence, Kansas", "Lawrence, KS"). The config refuses a place that a region also lists, and the catch-all: a place is inside or outside, never both.

The 58 stored events in Lawrence on 2026-11-20 (54 active, 4 unverified and held for promotion) were expired by hand in the commit that made the change, by the same function the run uses, so the site dropped them at that deploy rather than at the next run; the seven sources' run state went with them. The pick among them, the 102nd Vespers at the Lied Center, went too.

What stays: an event in the geography whose page happens to be a Lawrence institution's. The Spencer Museum's calendar listed a talk at the Clendening History of Medicine Library in Kansas City, Kansas; it is placed in Kansas City, Kansas, was not expired, and is re-verified from its own page as any event whose source left the registry is. Nothing will find the next such event, since the calendar is no longer read. That is the cost of the decision, and it is small: one event of the 59.

Alternatives considered. Keeping Lawrence for concerts alone, as first proposed, would have kept the misleading part (a Granada show under a Kansas City name) and needed a rule about kinds per region that nothing else has. Keeping the data and hiding the region on the site would have spent seven of the registry's sixty active sources on events no page shows. Dropping by the model's flag alone, with no `outsidePlaces`, is what Topeka gets and is enough for a place no source has ever been in; Lawrence had 58 live records and seven calendars that had been read every week, so it gets a rule in code. Any other place can be given the same treatment with one line of config.

If Lawrence comes back, it comes back as a registry decision: remove the lines from `outsidePlaces`, restore the region and the sources, and the next run reads the calendars afresh. The events expired as `outside-geography` would still turn their sightings away, whatever the config said by then, so the same change would clear that reason from the ones still to come.
